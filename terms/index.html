BBRG Back Office: Terms of Use

Effective October 14, 2026

BBRG Back Office is an internal tool operated by Board & Brew Restaurant Group for its own business records. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

Access is limited to QuickBooks companies owned or managed by Board & Brew and authorized by an owner. The tool reads accounting data to produce internal reports.

Data is used only for Board & Brew's internal operations and is not sold or shared with third parties. See the Board & Brew privacy policy.

Board & Brew may change or discontinue the tool at any time.
